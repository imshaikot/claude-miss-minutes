Miss Minutes
============

Drag "Miss Minutes" into Applications, then open it. She appears on your
desktop and a little clock shows up in the menu bar.

Requirements
- macOS 14 or later
- Claude Code installed and signed in (`claude` on your PATH): she uses it as her brain.
- Node.js (optional): lets Claude move her, read your screen and set reminders.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. The first time, right-click the app
and choose Open, or run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Miss Minutes.app"

Talk to her: click her, or press Control-Option-M anywhere.
